Ayadati opens in Lebanon

Ayadati is now open to clinics across Lebanon, with the interface available in Arabic, English and French from the first day rather than as a later translation pass.

A clinic that joins gets scheduling, a patient record that stays current, secure messaging and telehealth in one place. Staff see the schedule for their own location; patients see their own appointments, results and documents.

What is available now

  • Appointment booking and reminders, including SMS and WhatsApp delivery
  • A patient record that follows the person between visits
  • Lab and prescription documents attached to the visit they belong to
  • Role-based access, so each member of the team sees only what their work requires

What comes next

We are working through insurance claim handling and deeper laboratory integrations. Clinics already on the platform help decide what we build first.

If you run a clinic and want to see it working with your own schedule, get in touch and we will walk you through it.
