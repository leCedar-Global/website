Service Providers and Subprocessors

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

This page lists the third parties involved in delivering the Ayadati platform, and the role each one plays. It is referenced by our Privacy Policy and by the Data Processing Agreement we enter into with each clinic.

The distinction matters. A subprocessor is engaged where we are ourselves acting as a processor for a clinic. A processor is engaged where we are the controller. An independent controller decides its own purposes and is not acting on our instructions at all.

1. Subprocessors for clinic and patient data

These process data held in a clinic workspace, on the clinic's instructions passed through us. The Article 28 notice and objection rights below apply to this table.

SubprocessorLegal entityWhat it processesWhere
Microsoft Azure (compute, Cosmos DB, Blob Storage)Microsoft Ireland Operations LimitedAll platform data, including clinic and patient records, documents and account dataEuropean Union
Azure Communication ServicesMicrosoft Ireland Operations LimitedVideo consultation sessions, in-app chat, SMS and WhatsApp messages, and recipient phone numbersMicrosoft EU Data Boundary (EU and EFTA). Messages to recipients outside the boundary are delivered through external carrier networks.
Azure OpenAI ServiceMicrosoft Ireland Operations LimitedText submitted for optional AI assistance inside a clinic workspace, such as drafting or summarisingSweden Central, a regional deployment inside the European Union
Azure AI Document IntelligenceMicrosoft Ireland Operations LimitedDocuments uploaded to a patient record, to extract their contents. Microsoft holds submitted documents and results briefly, normally up to 24 hours, to return the result.Sweden Central, European Union
Azure AI SpeechMicrosoft Ireland Operations LimitedDictated audio, where a clinic uses dictation, to produce textSweden Central, European Union

2. Processors and independent controllers for our own data

We are the controller for the data below, so these are not subprocessors of clinic data.

ProviderLegal entityRoleWhat it processesWhere
Google AnalyticsGoogle Ireland LimitedOur processorPublic website and booking page usage, only after the visitor consents, and never on the patient portal or clinic dashboardEuropean Union, with onward transfers to the United States
WhishWHISH MONEY S.A.L.Independent controllerPayment transactions for subscriptions. Whish determines its own purposes for the payment data it holds, under its own terms and privacy notice.Lebanon

Lebanon is not covered by a European Commission adequacy decision. Personal data transferred to WHISH MONEY S.A.L. for payment processing therefore relies on an appropriate Chapter V transfer mechanism, together with a transfer assessment and any additional measures that assessment requires. Clinics can request details at support@ayadati.org.

3. Transfers outside the EU or EEA

Where a provider transfers personal data outside the EU or EEA, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision by the European Commission or on the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional technical measures the specific transfer requires. Clinics can request the safeguard applicable to a particular transfer by emailing support@ayadati.org.

4. Changes to subprocessors

Before adding or replacing a subprocessor in section 1, we will update this page and notify clinic account holders, so that a clinic has an opportunity to object before the change takes effect.

Questions about this page should go to support@ayadati.org.
